I’m a Ph.D. Candidate in International Relations at the University of Pécs in Hungary. I also hold a Master’s degree and a Bachelor’s degree in International Relations and Global Security from the Ural Federal University, both in Russia. A dedicated, detailed, and capable research fellow with 7 years in research. Extensively published in social science journals. A confident presenter at conferences, able to explain complex information to audiences of various levels.

My research interests cover the subnational dimension of global politics and role of non-state actors toward changes of international orders. In addition, the nuclear energy domain occupies a strong share in my investigations.  Specifically, I am interested in the network approach of International Relations, Paradiplomacy, and Comparative Regionalism with the focus on the geopolitical dimension.

Research interests 
======
* Comparative Regionalism
* Paradiplomacy Studies
* Nuclear Energy
* Geopolitics
* Non-state Actors
